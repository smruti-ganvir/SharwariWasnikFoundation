About Udyogini

Udyogini is a new initiative under the Sharvari Wasnik Foundation, established to utilize the inherent skills of underprivileged women and empower them to create meaningful social change.

Operating as a traditional Gruh Udyog (cottage industry), we currently manufacture and commercialize fresh Jowar, Bajra, and Makka flours. 100% of the income and profits generated from these sales are directly directed toward educating underprivileged women and young girl children.

We are actively expanding to bring authentic local flavors to the commercial market by tapping into the culinary expertise of Vidarbhi women—producing traditional flours, seasonal papads, achars, and other Vidarbhi delicacies. Furthermore, we are extending into stitching and tailoring ventures by providing sewing machines and professional training to help women gain financial independence through their craft.
